2. What the app is

Darkroom is a guide for developing and printing photographic film at home: timers, datasheet times, chemistry tracking, a light meter, a light planner, a negative viewer and a log. It gives guidance only. It is not professional, safety or medical advice.

3. Safety

Photographic chemicals can irritate skin, eyes and lungs, and some are harmful if swallowed. You are responsible for handling them safely: read the safety data sheet for each product, wear gloves and eye protection, work in a well-ventilated room, keep chemicals away from children, pets and food, and dispose of them as your local rules say.

Results such as negatives, slides and prints can vary, and the app cannot guarantee any result.
